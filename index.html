TapCount

A simple, private tap counter for iPhone and Android.

Support

Found a bug or have a question? Write to cla.alessandro01@gmail.com and include your device model and app version (Settings → About).

Premium

TapCount Premium is a one-time purchase that unlocks the home-screen widget and inactivity reminders. If you change phone, open Settings and tap "Restore purchase" while signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account.

Your data

Everything stays on your device. Use Settings → Data → Export data to save a JSON copy of your counters, and Import data to bring it back.
